A license with a reason behind it.

Elevated Dreams holds a CAURD license: New York’s Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary license.

01

What CAURD means

CAURD is New York's first-round adult-use retail license, created for people and families harmed by past cannabis enforcement.

02

Who runs Elevated Dreams

Elevated Dreams is owner-operated by Shawn Campbell. It is not a chain or a multi-state operator. License number OCM-CAURD-26-000353.

03

What it took to open

Shawn Campbell paid rent for 13 months on a store he could not open, while the town and the state each waited on the other. He never quit. Read the full story.
