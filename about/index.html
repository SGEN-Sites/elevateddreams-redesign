Thirteen months of locked doors. Then the doors opened.

Elevated Dreams LLC is a Newburgh dispensary focused on premium products, safe education, and a smooth customer experience. We believe cannabis should be approachable, stigma-free, and curated for your lifestyle.

Built by an owner who would not quit.

Elevated Dreams holds a CAURD license: New York's first-round adult-use retail license, created for people and families harmed by past cannabis enforcement. It is owner-operated by Shawn Campbell. It is not a chain or a multi-state operator.

RENT PAID · MONTH 01

Thirteen months, one floor at a time

  1. LOBBY · Lobby

    A signed lease. A locked door.

    Unit 10, Algonquin Plaza, 190 South Plank Road. The rent was due every month. The doors could not open.

  2. FLOOR 4 · May 1, 2025

    The Planning Board.

    The Town of Newburgh treated the store as a special use. It came before the Planning Board on May 1, 2025.

  3. FLOOR 7 · August 7, 2025

    A public hearing.

    A public hearing followed on August 7, 2025. The rent kept coming due.

  4. FLOOR 13 · December 2025

    Thirteen months of rent.

    Shawn Campbell told the state Cannabis Control Board he had paid rent for 13 months without being able to open. The town would not issue a building permit without full state licensure. The state wanted the town’s certificate of occupancy first.

  5. PENTHOUSE · 2026

    The doors open.

    License OCM-CAURD-26-000353. Elevated Dreams is open on South Plank Road. He never quit.
